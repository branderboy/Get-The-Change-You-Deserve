Phase Three

The Organic Content System

Designed to maximize D.C.-market impressions, familiarity, and recognition before requesting donations or votes.

Podcast & Video Clipping

A 40-minute interview becomes 15-40 usable assets. Repackaging core messages with direct, accountability, or community hooks to create multiple entry points.

TikTok & Reels

Top-of-funnel discovery platforms built around one tension at a time. Formats include direct-to-camera, street conversations, and myth-vs-fact content.

Facebook Hub

Facebook serves as the community library for longer content and feeds viewers to the campaign website. Selected clips are strategically seeded into established D.C. culture, neighborhood, and news pages.

Neighborhood Conversations

Unscripted "Man-on-the-street" content showing Hamilton listening to real concerns outside of a podium setting, proving she can answer without political jargon.
