Content Engine

Podcast → Clips → Library → Posting

Part of Social Media$2,150

The clipping campaign works from video you already have. Your weekly podcast, past interviews, speeches, event footage and any other recorded conversations become a steady flow of short videos. Nothing new has to be shot to start.

  • The flow. Long-form recording, then clips, then an organized library, then scheduled posts, then a performance review.
  • How we pick. We review every recording, mark the strongest moments, and brief the clippers on each one, with the timestamp, the hook, the caption and the platform.
  • The themes. Who you are, why you are running, independent leadership, D.C. accountability, and the issues residents raise.

How we pick the clips

We review the long-form footage, mark the strongest moments, and hand the editors and clippers a brief for each one, so nobody is cutting at random. Each brief includes:

  • Video source and timestamp
  • Topic and content category
  • Hook and suggested caption
  • Recommended platform
  • Ideal video length
  • Priority and approval status
