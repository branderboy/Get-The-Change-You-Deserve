Phase Three

The Organic Content System

Each asset feeds the next: recordings become clips, clips fill the folder, the folder feeds posters, and views hand off to the website. The winners become ads.

01

Podcast Clipping Campaign

Every podcast appearance, interview, and speech becomes a library. One 40-minute interview yields 15 to 40 usable assets.

  • Position Clips. Based on 13 core categories.
  • Four Hooks. Direct, Accountability, Community, Independent.
  • Tools. Google Drive + Opus Clip.

Hamilton’s podcast appearances, interviews, recorded conversations, speeches, and long-form discussions will be divided into short, position-based clips.

The goal is to make one long-form recording produce multiple audience-entry points.

02

Content Folder

One shared folder is the distribution engine: every clipper and volunteer downloads from it and posts daily.

  • One Link. Anyone with the link can post without asking.
  • Scheduled. Buffer schedules posts up until the election.

A central repository for all assets. Organized by week with captions and tags included.

03

TikTok & Reels Discovery

The top of the funnel. One question or tension per clip, curiosity in the first 3 seconds. Never a speech or commercial.

  • 13 Core Questions. Addressing the actual concerns discovered in the audit.
  • 10 Organic Formats. Direct-to-camera, street interviews, explainers, swipe posts, etc.
  • Direct Voice. It feels like Rhonda Hamilton speaking to one person with a real question about D.C.

TikTok and Instagram Reels will serve as the primary top-of-funnel discovery platforms. The content will be direct, conversational, understandable, visual, and built around one question or tension at a time.

04

FB Campaign Content Hub

The long-form social library. Residents watch longer content, revisit a topic, comment in detail, and share inside family networks.

  • What It Holds. Podcast clips, longer interviews, explainers, polls, feedback, updates, events, and reposts.
  • Where It Sends People. The campaign website remains the owned destination for positions, signups, and donations.

Facebook’s role is not limited to viral discovery. It gives residents a place to watch longer content, revisit a topic, comment in more detail, and share content inside family, neighborhood, and community networks.

05

Local Theme-Page Distribution

Clips placed inside the D.C. pages residents already spend attention on: music, culture, lifestyle, neighborhood news, and civic discussion.

  • Selection Rule. Local audience quality over follower count. Real engagement over inflated likes.
  • Native, Not Forced. Posts feel like the page, not political advertising.

The purpose is to introduce Hamilton inside content environments where residents already spend attention, expanding visibility beyond traditional campaign pages.

06

Neighborhood Conversations

Hamilton outside the podium, the studio, and the press release: answering real residents on location. Loose, human, clear, watchable.

  • Formats. Walk-throughs, resident questions, rapid-response rounds.
  • The Test. Prepared but not over-rehearsed. Answering without political language.

The tone should feel loose, human, clear, and watchable. Hamilton should sound prepared but not over-rehearsed, answering questions without hiding behind political language.

The Destination

Campaign Website & Progressive App

Everything above sends people somewhere. This is where they land, and where attention turns into a poll answer, a contact, and a donation.

07

Website, Mobile, & PWA

One build delivered three ways: a website, a mobile experience, and an app residents install from the browser. The flow is capture the poll, capture the contact, then donate.

  • Better Quality. A site that looks like the campaign, not a template. Fast on a phone, installable to the home screen.
  • Messaging Match. Every clip and ad lands on a page that reinforces the exact same message.
  • Data Capture. One-tap polling with email/SMS opt-in to build the retargeting list.
  • ManyChat Flow. Automated donations and support handled inside IG DMs and FB Messenger.
