## 3. Phase One — The Sentiment Audit

Three research streams, each with a named output.

- [ ] **3.1 Google Trends audit** — what the D.C. area is actively searching for (cost of living, crime, Pepco bills, 311 complaints, youth curfews), rising questions, news-driven spikes.
  **Output:** Search-interest report
- [ ] **3.2 Polling data review** — issue priorities, confidence in local government, candidate awareness, demographic differences in voter attitudes.
  **Output:** High-priority issues list
- [ ] **3.3 Public conversations scan (Reddit and social)** — actual language, jokes, misunderstandings, and emotional reactions polling misses (r/washingtondc, ward discussions, news comments).
  **Output:** D.C. Language & Conversation Guide

---

## 4. Phase Two — Convert Audit Into Messaging

- [ ] **4.1 Central brand position** locked and applied across all content: *"Promises are easy. Follow-through is the proof."*
- [ ] **4.2 Issue content packages** — one repeatable package per major issue identified in Phase One. Each package contains four parts:
  1. Public Question
  2. Hamilton Perspective
  3. Short-form Hook
  4. Poll Question
- [ ] **4.3 First package: City Reliability** (the plan's worked example)
  - Public Question: "Why does it feel like residents have to fight for basic city services?"
  - Hamilton Perspective: "Government should not make residents chase the same problem over and over. The city should show the timeline, the agency, and the result."
  - Short-form Hook: "How long should it take D.C. to fix a basic problem?"
  - Poll Question: "What issue do you report most often: trash, parking, streetlights, potholes, rats?"

## 5. Phase Three — The Organic Content System

Four production lanes plus the funnel they feed.

- [ ] **5.1 Podcast Clipping** — long-form interviews cut into short, position-based clips. Target yield: 15 to 40 assets per 40-minute interview. Each message repackaged with four hooks: Direct, Accountability, Community, Independent Identity.
- [ ] **5.2 Discovery Content (TikTok / Reels)** — one tension per piece, never a speech. Formats: Direct-to-camera, Street conversations, Issue explainers, Polls, Swipe posts, Myth-vs-Fact.
- [ ] **5.3 Long-Form Hub (Facebook)** — social library and community-response channel that feeds viewers to the campaign website for official positions, donations, and voter information.
- [ ] **5.4 Neighborhood Conversations** — man-on-the-street content: rapid-response questions, walk-throughs, answering real residents.

### 5.5 Organic Distribution Funnel (tracking deliverable)

| Stage | Objective | Outcome to track |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] Exposure | Reels, TikToks, theme pages in front of D.C. audiences | D.C. impressions and reach |
